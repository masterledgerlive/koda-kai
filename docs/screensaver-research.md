# Screensaver Golden Age — Overlay Tricks Research 🌩️

*For Kodakai's fun overlay look. Compiled 2026-10-03 from web research.*
*Rule of the era: the best screensavers didn't decorate the screen — they played gags ON it.*

---

## The thunderstorm (David's core memory)

### After Dark — "Rain Storm" (Berkeley Systems, After Dark for Windows 1.0)
- What it did: a storm appears **on the screen itself**, complete with wind and lightning.
- Steal this for Kodakai: Drop transforms into a dark cartoon cloud with hands and **throws lightning bolts across the page** — each strike leaves a scorch/burn-mark overlay, then he wipes the page clean with a giggle.

### After Dark — "Hard Rain"
- What it did: raindrops hit the screen and left **colorful circular splats** all over it.
- Steal this for Kodakai: clouds multiply across the top of the screen, then dozens of little Drops **rain down and dance onto the desktop**, each leaving a ripple where it lands.

## Desktop-eating gags (the screen is the playground)

### After Dark — "Can of Worms"
- Worms emerge from the screen, crawl around, and **"eat" the screen content**.
- Steal: Drop's rain leaves little puddle-Drops that "drink" parts of the page, then burp them back.

### After Dark — "Chameleon"
- Chameleons walk across the screen, changing colors and **eating desktop icons**.
- Steal: a chameleon-Drop that changes color per page section and "nibbles" buttons (they pop right back).

### After Dark — "Bugs"
- Digital bugs crawl across the screen.
- Steal: ladybug Drops marching across in a line, waving as they pass.

### After Dark — "Punch Out"
- The desktop is **attacked by a hole puncher** (optional clicking sound).
- Steal: Drop punches confetti holes in the page during celebrations.

### After Dark — "Boris" / "Bad Dog"
- Boris: a good cat plays on the desktop and chases butterflies. Bad Dog: the opposite.
- Steal: Drop chases butterfly-cursors around the screen; a "Bad Drop" mode for April Fools.

### After Dark — "Down the Drain" / "Bulge"
- The desktop spirals down a drain; the screen appears to bulge like jelly.
- Steal: page swirls down a drain when switching sections; Drop bulges the screen when he bonks into it.

## The storytelling screensaver

### Johnny Castaway (Sierra/Dynamix, 1992) — "the world's first story-telling screen saver"
- What it did: a castaway on a tiny island; randomized vignettes (fishing, sandcastles, mermaid visits, Lilliputian pirates, a seagull stealing his shorts while bathing), a day/night cycle tied to the system clock, holiday Easter eggs (jack-o'-lantern on Halloween, "Happy New Year" banner), and endless near-rescue gags — you had to stop working to catch the story.
- Steal: Drop's overlay life runs on **vignettes + a day/night cycle** — he fishes at noon, sleeps at night, celebrates holidays, and the kid never sees the same show twice.

## The overlay assistants (desktop pets)

### Shimeji (Group Finity, open source)
- What it did: little mascots that walk the screen, **climb walls and window edges**, sit, get dragged and tossed by the cursor; actions defined in simple XML so anyone could make new characters.
- Steal: Drop climbs the edges of the app and perches on buttons; the XML-style action list becomes Kodakai's open **moves list** that the community can extend.

### Microsoft Agent / BonziBuddy (1999–2004)
- What it did: a cartoon assistant living on the desktop that told jokes, sang, and talked with text-to-speech. BonziBuddy became infamous as adware/spyware — the cautionary tale.
- Steal: the *charm* (jokes, songs, TTS buddy on the desktop), never the sins — Kodakai stays kid-safe, local-first, and honest about what it does.

## Screen pranks & destruction

### Broken-screen prank apps (modern phone era)
- What they do: touch/shake/timer triggers a realistic cracked-glass overlay on top of everything, with fire and electric variants; one tap removes it.
- Steal: Drop's lightning leaves **crack overlays** radiating from the strike point — pure theater, one tap (or his wave) wipes them away.

### After Dark — "Hall of Mirrors" / "Mondrian" / "Mosaic"
- The screen reflects infinitely / becomes a Mondrian painting / becomes a mosaic of itself.
- Steal: "art attack" mode — Drop turns the page into a watercolor, then shakes it back.

---

## Playable & interactive

### After Dark — "Lunatic Fringe" / "Mime Hunt"
- A playable space shooter / a mime hunt with crosshairs **inside the screensaver**.
- Steal: overlay mini-games — catch the falling Drops, tap the lightning before it lands.

### After Dark — "Message Mayhem"
- A figure walks on screen and **scrawls out a message**.
- Steal: Drop writes the kid's name (or the word of the day) across the screen in water.

---

## The pattern to copy

Every beloved one did the same trick: **the screen is not a stage, it's a victim and a playmate.** Things land ON it, eat it, crack it, paint it, climb its edges — and then it all wipes clean like nothing happened. Kodakai's overlay look = water-edition of exactly that: storms, rain, splats, cracks, and a little blue guy with cartoon hands doing all of it.
